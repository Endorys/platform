# Community conduct

Endorys welcomes respectful technical disagreement and contributions across engineering, research and clinical disciplines.

Be considerate, credit others' work, distinguish evidence from speculation and critique ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate misrepresentation and disclosure of others' private information are unacceptable.

Discuss scientific concerns openly through issues with evidence. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer at sadhu.shobab@gmail.com with the relevant context; avoid exposing private details publicly. The maintainer may request revisions, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation according to severity and recurrence. The maintainer should explain substantive moderation decisions where privacy permits.

This is a project-specific conduct policy; it does not claim certification or adoption of another organization's governance.
