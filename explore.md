|---|---|---|

| C03 | Tendon and ligament injury | Directional mechanics, loading response and recovery tracking |

| C08 | Liver and deep-organ fibrosis | Accessible acoustic/mechanical signatures, depth/window limitations and repeatability |

## Shared assessment dimensions

Acoustic response, mechanical response, directional/solid-fluid models, electrical response, physiological context, geometry/contact, longitudinal change and decision evidence. Define the user, decision, geometry, reference measurement, confounders, uncertainty and next experiment for each opportunity.

## Evidence status

Research concept. Each proposed measurement, inference or intervention requires task-specific validation. Naming does not establish performance or readiness.
