# Endorys Challenge

Critical review, falsification and failure analysis.

## Workstreams

- Identifiability and competing explanations
- Failure modes and confounders
- Sensor ablation and baseline comparison
- Negative results and stop criteria
- Model robustness and uncertainty
- Critique of clinical and commercial assumptions
