# Endorys Bench

Physical experiments and prototype testing.

## Workstreams

- Source and component characterization
- Acoustic routing and distributed receiver experiments
- Phantoms and controlled mechanical perturbations
- Multimodal synchronization and registration
- Repeatability and confounder sensitivity
- Reference measurements, raw data and reproducible analysis

## Evidence status

Research concept. Each proposed measurement, inference or intervention requires task-specific validation. Naming does not establish performance or readiness.

[All branches](BRANCH_REGISTER.md)


## Development focus

Build a reproducible direct acoustic baseline, then introduce routing, geometry, mechanics and complementary sensing sequentially.
