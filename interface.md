# Endorys Interface

Mechanical tissue interrogation, coupling, sensor interfaces and disposable components.

## Workstreams

- Controlled mechanical tissue interrogation
- Pressure, strain, creep, relaxation and hysteresis
- Coupling and acoustic contact interfaces
- Disposable sensing/interrogation components
- Dressings, strips and sleeves
- Contact calibration and reapplication studies

## Scope evolution

The original mechanical tissue programme and later disposable sensing/interrogation ecosystem both remain here. Matrix covers the integrated sensing surface; Interface covers contact components and controlled mechanical interrogation.

## Evidence status

Research concept. Each proposed measurement, inference or intervention requires task-specific validation. Naming does not establish performance or readiness.

## Development focus

Calibrate applied load, displacement, coupling and boundary conditions. Disposable concepts need material, process and intended-use definition.
