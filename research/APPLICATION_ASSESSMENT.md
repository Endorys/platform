# Application assessment

The first application must identify a particular measurement and decision. Broad organ lists are opportunity maps, not indications.

| Candidate | Useful research question | Major limitation/comparator |
|---|---|---|
| Breast | Can controlled response measurements add reproducible information? | Depth, coupling, heterogeneity and comparison with established breast assessment pathways |
| Superficial muscle/tendon | Can response under controlled loading be reproduced? | Anisotropy, activation, boundary conditions and reference mechanics/imaging |
| Vascular | Can a predefined flow or wall-response quantity be measured? | Motion, pressure dependence, geometry and validated vascular reference |
| Abdomen | Is an acoustic window adequate for a specific target? | Depth, gas, motion and limited access; organ visibility cannot be assumed |
| Surgical assistance | Can a registered measurement support a defined intraoperative task? | Sterility, access, deformation, workflow and clinical outcomes |
| Materials/NDT | Can a known specimen property or defect be distinguished? | Material-specific propagation and independent destructive/metrology reference |

Breast was an original motivation. It remains part of the research scope; no breast diagnostic claim is established. Superficial phantoms may be a more practical first measurement environment. Materials results cannot be transferred directly to human tissue.

The [Explore register](../explore.md) preserves clinical and materials branches; [Therapy](../therapy.md) and [Surgical](../surgical.md) have distinct validation burdens. Select candidates using access, controllable confounders, reference availability and decision value. No numerical market or clinical performance score is justified by current bench evidence.
