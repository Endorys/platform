# Governance and decisions

Founder and current maintainer: Sadhu Shobab Bollipo. Endorys is a research initiative; these pages do not assert incorporation, regulatory approval, institutional sponsorship or endorsement by a former employer.

Use issues for questions and proposals, pull requests for reviewable changes, and dated decision records for architectural choices. A decision record should state context, alternatives, evidence, decision, consequences and conditions for reconsideration. Preserve historical names in migration records, while using current names in new work.

Credit contributions by their actual scope. Cite external research near the claims it supports. Corrections should identify what changed and why. The canonical branch register governs branch naming; an attractive illustration cannot override the evidence register.
