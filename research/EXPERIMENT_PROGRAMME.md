# Proposed experiment programme

| ID | Investigation | Main confounder/control | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| E001 | Direct acoustic phantom baseline | Coupling, geometry, temperature, electronic delay | Repeatability and uncertainty report |
| E002 | Source routing | Direct reference, path length, leakage, settling | Per-path transfer function and switching limits |
| E003 | Conformal geometry | Independent position reference and reseating | Pose/geometry error propagation |
| E004 | Controlled mechanical response | Calibrated loading and boundary conditions | One reference-checked parameter |
| E005 | Time-dependent mechanics | Loading history, recovery and temperature | Creep/relaxation model comparison |
| E006 | Complementary electrical sensing | Electrode contact and spatial sensitivity | Incremental value against ultrasound alone |
| E007 | Reapplication | Operator, day and specimen effects | Between-session uncertainty |
| E008 | Failure detection | Known blank, saturation, timing and contact faults | Detection and false-rejection results |

Each experiment needs a frozen protocol, raw data, calibration, analysis version and results. These are planned investigations, not completed demonstrations. Human-use and therapy studies require a separate programme and appropriate oversight.
