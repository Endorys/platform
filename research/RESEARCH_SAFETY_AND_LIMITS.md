# Research scope and limits

The current programme begins with non-biological phantoms and suitably rated laboratory equipment. It does not provide a protocol for diagnosing or treating people. Concept images of wearable, breast, surgical or therapy applications are proposed scenarios, not evidence that a device is safe or effective.

Before hardware operation, identify electrical, acoustic, mechanical, thermal and interference hazards against the actual equipment and experiment. Use qualified review where needed. Do not infer safe acoustic exposure from a frequency label or low controller power. Human, animal, clinical and therapeutic work requires its own appropriate oversight, controls and validation.

No numerical exposure limit, approval pathway or clinical threshold is prescribed here. Those depend on intended use, jurisdiction, standards and actual device characteristics.
