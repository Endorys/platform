# Endorys Vascular

Vascular and procedural application programme.

## Workstreams

- Vascular wall mechanics and flow-referenced observations
- Controlled compressibility and clot research
- Aneurysm response and change tracking
- Defined vascular procedural events
- Ultrasound-led endovascular application research
- Procedure-specific reference tests and failure detection
