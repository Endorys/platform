# Deterministic Coupling Quality Index (CQI) Gating for Operator-Independent Quantitative Ultrasound on Non-Planar Surfaces
Author: Sadhu Shobab Bollipo (M/s Endorys, Bengaluru, India)
Archival Software and Benchmark DOI: https://doi.org
Patent Reference: Indian Patent Application No. 202641134091 (Priority Date: 07 November 2026)

Abstract:
Quantitative ultrasound (QUS) biomarkers suffer from poor inter-session reproducibility on non-planar anatomical and industrial surfaces due to interfacial air gaps and operator contact pressure. We present a closed-loop conformable array validation framework (ENDORYS E001) combining an impedance-matched assisted-coupling interface (~1.50 MRayl) with deterministic sequential pitch-catch Coupling Quality Index (CQI) gating. In 2D FDTD (k-Wave) benchmarks at 1.7 MHz across a curved tissue-mimicking phantom (28 mm radius, 1540 m/s background, 1585 m/s inclusion), 3 mm solid-hydrogel assisted coupling preserves >75% of subsurface inclusion echo amplitude (+/-4.37 kPa at 15.6-16.3 us), whereas a 0.6 mm interfacial air-gap fault produces a trapped boundary reflection spike (+/-10.2 kPa at 5.5-6.5 us) triggering automated frame rejection. Aligned with AIUM-RSNA QIBA PEQUS, IEC 60601-2-37:2024, and ASTM E1316 / ISO 16810.

Reproducibility: Open simulation datasets (e001_sim_assisted.csv, e001_sim_unassisted.csv), HDF5 .uff loaders, and repeatability scoring scripts are archived at https://doi.org.
