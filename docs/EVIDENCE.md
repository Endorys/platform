# Evidence and validation guide

[Documentation home](README.md) · [Status](STATUS.md)

## Assess the claims

1. Read the [28-item proof register](../research/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md): each item separates external precedent from the Endorys-specific gap.
2. Consult the [literature review](../research/LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). Its original review date is 16 October 2026; it is bounded, not exhaustive or patent clearance.
3. Read the [technical evaluation](../research/TECHNICAL_EVALUATION.md) for confounders and engineering tradeoffs. Current branch registers take precedence over its historical taxonomy.
4. Evaluate [application candidates](../research/APPLICATION_ASSESSMENT.md) against a specific decision and comparator.

## Produce new evidence

Use the [roadmap](../research/ROADMAP.md), [experiment programme](../research/EXPERIMENT_PROGRAMME.md) and [E001 protocol](../research/EXPERIMENT_001.md). Freeze the question, independent units, controls and decision criteria before confirmatory data collection.

Publish a [result record](../research/RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md) with raw-data provenance, calibration, uncertainty, code version, deviations and negative findings. The [results register](../research/RESULTS_AND_NEGATIVE_FINDINGS.md) currently records the absence of integrated physical evidence.

## Review questions

- Can coupling, geometry or temperature explain the result?
- Are repeated traces mistaken for independent specimens?
- Is the inverse parameter identifiable under the stated assumptions?
- Does sensor fusion improve held-out performance over ultrasound alone?
- Are a failed acquisition and a negative biological result distinguished?
- Is the reference itself calibrated and appropriate?

Use an evidence-correction issue to challenge a claim. Preserve an audit trail when correcting published conclusions.
