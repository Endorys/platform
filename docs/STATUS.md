## Evidence vocabulary

- **Proposed:** a design or hypothesis; no experiment implied.
- **Synthetic:** generated data under stated assumptions; no physical proof implied.
- **Physical bench:** inspectable apparatus, calibration and raw non-biological records.
- **Biological:** appropriately overseen studies with biological material.
- **Clinical:** intended-use evaluation in the relevant population and setting.

A published precedent belongs to its original authors and apparatus. It does not upgrade Endorys's evidence level. An image depicting a monitor is not a recorded Endorys measurement.
