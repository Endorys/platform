# ENDORYS Technical Disclosure v0.1.1: Alternative Coupling and Transducer Modalities for Conformable QUS
Author: Sadhu Shobab Bollipo (M/s Endorys, Bengaluru, India) | Date: November 26, 2026
Patent Reference: Indian Patent Application No. 202641134091 | DOI: https://doi.org

Purpose: Timestamped open defensive publication establishing prior art on alternative physical implementations so third parties cannot patent narrow hardware workarounds:
1. Magnetic and Electromagnetic Retention Pucks: Flexible ferromagnetic elastomer strips or opposing magnetic backing rings seating conformable piezo elements on curved metallic pipes or anatomical cuffs.
2. Micro-Pneumatic and Electro-Active Polymer Actuators: Per-element micro-bladders or dielectric elastomer actuators adjusting local standoff height dynamically from sequential CQI feedback.
3. CMUT / PMUT Flexible Silicon-on-Polymer Arrays: Capacitive or piezoelectric micromachined ultrasonic transducer dies mounted on notched polyimide flex-PCBs interrogated via the ENDORYS sequential pitch-catch CQI protocol.
