# Endorys Surgical

Surgical and procedural tissue-awareness research.

## Workstreams

- Conventional open abdominal surgery assistance
- Intraoperative functional tissue awareness
- Lesion/margin localization and pathology registration research
- Biopsy and procedural guidance research
- Perfusion-related response and tissue-viability research
- Instrument/anatomical registration
- Motion, coupling and sterile-interface integration

## Provenance

These workstreams organize recovered surgical/procedural discussions. A dedicated original exhaustive surgical subbranch register has not been recovered. Conventional open abdominal surgery remains included.
