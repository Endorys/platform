## Hierarchy and completeness

Explore retains 35 historical clinical discovery categories, 16 overlapping clinical application records and 9 materials/NDT records. Do not add these as independent indications. Therapy retains 18 historical categories. Other workstreams organize the available recovered scope. The first original discussion and an exhaustive original surgical register remain historical gaps.

## Working relationships

Matrix and Interface support acquisition and controlled interrogation. Insight interprets observations. Continuum retains data and integration context. Explore identifies opportunities. Therapy, Surgical and Vascular define application programmes. Bench provides physical tests, Evidence assesses results and Challenge probes competing explanations and failures.
