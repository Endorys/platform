# Endorys Platform

Overall ultrasound-led multiphysics tissue research platform.

## Workstreams

- Shared acoustic source selection and passive routing
- One active source/path/contact at a time
- Distributed receive transducers and synchronized AFE/ADC
- External computation and acquisition-aware inference
- Controlled perturbation, response and recovery
- Supporting modalities chosen for each intended use

## Evidence status

Research concept. Each proposed measurement, inference or intervention requires task-specific validation. Naming does not establish performance or readiness.
